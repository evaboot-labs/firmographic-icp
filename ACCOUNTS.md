# Accounts: the deals you won and the deals you lost

Status: EXAMPLE

Your AI Agent reads the firmographics of each account through Evaboot, then compares the won ones with the lost ones. Pull them from your CRM: closed-won deals and closed-lost deals of the last 12 to 24 months. 20 of each gives a real signal; fewer gives a first guess.

The example rows are ten public companies used as stand-ins, with made-up outcomes. They are not anyone's customers.
